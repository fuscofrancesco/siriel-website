Privacy policy

Siriel collects nothing.

Siriel has no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking, on the Mac, iPhone or iPad. I don't receive your notes, your settings or anything about how you use the app. This page covers the apps and this website, and says exactly which requests they make and why.

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Your notes

Your notes are Markdown files in a folder you choose. Siriel reads and writes them there and nowhere else. If that folder is in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or anything else that syncs, the sync is between you and that service, under its terms.

AI

AI features run only when you ask: a proofread, a rewrite, a question in the chat. Siriel then sends the text that task needs straight from your device to the provider you picked, using your own key or account. Siriel doesn't relay it, keep a copy, or see it. What the provider does with it is governed by that provider's terms, not mine.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the providers are Anthropic, OpenAI and OpenRouter. Siriel asks before it sends anything to a provider for the first time, and the key stays in this device's Keychain.
  • On the Mac, you can also use Apple's on-device model or a local model, which send nothing anywhere, or the Claude Code and Codex command-line tools under your own account. Keys stay in Siriel's settings on your Mac, never in your notes folder.

Every request the apps make

Besides the AI requests above, these are the only ones:

  • Update check (Mac): asks GitHub whether a newer version of Siriel has been published.
  • Link previews (Mac): fetches the page a link points to, to show its title. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Model downloads: the natural read-aloud voice (Mac) and the voice transcription model (iPhone and iPad) download from Hugging Face, and only after you press Download. Both then run on your device, offline.

As with any connection, the service at the other end sees your IP address. Siriel sends it nothing else about you.

Microphone, camera and photos

On iPhone and iPad, Siriel asks for the microphone to record voice notes, and for the camera or photos to add a picture to a note. Recordings are transcribed on your device. The recording, its text and any photo are saved in your notes folder, like any other note, and go nowhere else.

What stays on your device

Settings, the search index and note history (kept for 30 days) are stored on your device, outside your notes folder. Removing the app removes them. Your notes stay where they are, because they are yours.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no web fonts from third parties. The Download button asks GitHub's public API which version is the latest, so GitHub sees that request. The company that hosts the site keeps the ordinary server logs any web host keeps.

Children

Siriel collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what the apps send ever changes, this page will change first, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Siriel is made by Francesco Fusco. Questions about privacy go to the issue tracker. It is public, so please don't include anything private there.
