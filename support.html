Help with Siriel.

Siriel is a free Markdown editor for the Mac, iPhone and iPad, made by one person. If something here doesn't answer your question, or something is broken, the issue tracker is the way to reach me.

Report a problem

Getting started

Siriel works on a folder of Markdown files, which it calls a vault. On the Mac, open any folder. On iPhone and iPad, tap Choose a folder and pick one in Files: an iCloud Drive folder gives you the same notes on every device. On a new device, you can also create an empty folder under On My iPhone or On My iPad.

AI features

AI features use the provider you choose, with your own API key: on iPhone and iPad, Proofread and Rewrite in the selection menu. On iPhone and iPad, add it under More → AI provider. Siriel asks once before it sends text to a provider. If a proofread says to allow sending text first, open that screen and press Save. Usage is billed by the provider, to your account.

Voice notes

On iPhone and iPad, voice notes are transcribed on your device. That needs a speech model, which Siriel downloads only after you agree, over Wi-Fi unless you allow cellular. Without it, voice notes are kept as audio. The model can be managed under More → Transcription.

Your data

Siriel has no account and collects nothing. Your notes are ordinary files in your folder, so they stay yours if you remove the app. The privacy policy lists every request the apps make.

Requirements

  • Mac: macOS on Apple Silicon.
  • iPhone and iPad: iOS and iPadOS 26 or later.

Reporting a problem

Open an issue on the issue tracker with your device, the version of Siriel, and what you did. It is public, so leave out anything private, including the contents of your notes.
